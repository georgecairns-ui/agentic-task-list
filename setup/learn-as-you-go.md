# Learn as you go

This file is the same in every Agentic tool. Pitch everything here at someone who has never opened Claude before.

## One tip per step (beginner mode)

In beginner mode, end each setup step with one short "You just learned" line: the one below that fits what they just did. Use each tip once, and skip this in the other guide modes.

| When | The line |
|---|---|
| They trusted the folder | You just learned: trusting a folder lets me update your notes and your app without asking every time. I still can't send or delete anything. |
| They talked an answer | You just learned: you can talk to me instead of typing. The microphone button in the message box works everywhere. |
| A connection worked | You just learned: a connection lets me read an app for you. You sign in on the app's own page, and I never see your password. |
| They showed you a screenshot | You just learned: when something on screen is confusing, paste a screenshot and I'll tell you where to click. |
| You built them a skill | You just learned: a skill is saved know-how. Once it's in Claude, I follow it every time without being told. |
| You told them a command | You just learned: type / in the message box to see your commands, for example /setup. |
| The app opened | You just learned: you can change anything on this page by asking me, for example "make the buttons green" or "add a box for unpaid invoices". |
| They told you something small that mattered | You just learned: tell me the small stuff as you would a colleague. It's how I get things right. |

## The 10-minute lesson at hand-over

At hand-over, in every guide mode, offer once:

> Would you like a 10-minute walkthrough of how to use Claude day to day? We'll do it with your own tools, right here.

With a yes, run it in this chat. 4 short parts, and they do each one themselves:

1. **Ask for your day.** They say the everyday phrase from `CLAUDE.md`'s skills table (for example "sort my day") and see what comes back.
2. **Get something drafted.** They ask for one real piece of work: a reply to an email, a chaser, a post or a first draft, whichever this tool does.
3. **Change the page.** They ask you to change one small thing in the app, and watch it happen.
4. **Talk it through.** They press the microphone and tell you, messily, something on their mind. You turn it into tasks or notes.

After each part, one line on what they just did and when they'd use it. Finish with: "That's it. Ask for anything in plain words, the way you'd ask a colleague."

If they say no, say: "Any time you'd like it, say 'show me how to use Claude'." Note the answer under "Notes for next time" in `setup/progress.md`.
